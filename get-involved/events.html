Past Events

Wolf Girls Twilight Movie Night

October 25 · Sacramento

On October 25th, our Wolf Girls came together in Sacramento for a cozy Twilight movie night under the evening sky! It was such a special time to connect with fellow members of our growing community, share stories, and welcome new faces. Everyone joined in on making Halloween crafts, enjoying treats, and learning more about the recent projects we’ve been working on to support wolves and conservation. We also talked about the different ways anyone can get involved. Nights like this remind us how strong our pack truly is when we come together with purpose and heart.

Wolf-Dog Hike with Bella & Chaos

October 25 · Local Trail

On October 25th, we hit the trails for our latest Wolf-Dog Hike! Our amazing wolf dogs, Bella and Chaos, joined us for a beautiful day out in nature alongside community members and hikers who came to walk, learn, and connect. The group enjoyed time on a local scenic trail, sharing stories and gaining a deeper appreciation for these incredible animals. It was a wonderful reminder of the bond between people, wolves, and the wild spaces we work to protect.

Paint a Wolf with Jackie

October 18 · Sacramento

On October 18th, we hosted Paint a Wolf with Jackie, a creative community event led by local Sacramento artist Jackie. Surrounded by nature, our pack gathered to paint, unwind, and express our love for wolves through art. It was such a peaceful and inspiring afternoon filled with laughter, connection, and creativity. Everyone brought their own unique touch to their wolf painting, celebrating both individuality and unity within our growing community. Events like these remind us that protecting wolves is not just about advocacy but also about celebrating their beauty and the spirit they represent.
