Rescue

At Women for Wolves, we are dedicated to rescuing and providing a safe haven for wolf-dogs who are at risk of being euthanized due to displacement or neglect. We place them at our sanctuary and also work closely with other trusted sanctuaries, offering a second chance for these misunderstood animals.

If you know of a wolf, wolf-dog, or dog in distress or in need of rescue, transport, or placement, please reach out to us — we are here to help and can provide the care they need.
